🔐 What is Phishing?

Phishing is a cyber attack where attackers pretend to be trusted organizations such as banks, delivery companies, universities, or official services to steal passwords, OTPs, card details, or personal data.

💀 How Phishing Works

  1. Attacker sends a fake message or suspicious link.
  2. User feels fear, urgency, greed, or pressure.
  3. User clicks a malicious link or shares private details.
  4. Account, OTP, money, or personal data is stolen.

🚨 Example Scam Message

URGENT: Your bank account is suspended!
Click http://fake-login.com
Enter your OTP immediately.

⚠️ Common Red Flags

Urgent Language Fake Links OTP Request Fear Tactics Prize Scam Account Blocked

🛡️ Safety Rules

  • Never share OTP or password with anyone.
  • Check website spelling before login.
  • Avoid unknown links and shortened URLs.
  • Enable two-factor authentication where possible.
  • Verify bank or official messages from trusted sources.
